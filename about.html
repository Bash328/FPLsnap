About FPLSnap

FPLSnap is a free, no-login tool for Fantasy Premier League managers. It shows upcoming fixture difficulty at a glance and loads fast on a phone.

It reads only the publicly available FPL API. We never ask for or store your FPL login. Ads keep it free.

FPLSnap is an independent tool and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to the Premier League or Fantasy Premier League.
