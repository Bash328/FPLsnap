Privacy policy

What we do and do not collect.

FPLSnap has no accounts and does not collect personal data itself. Your sort, gameweek, custom FDR, pinned teams, Team ID and transfer plans are stored only in your browser (localStorage) and are never sent to us.

When you enter a Team ID or league ID, your browser asks our API (a Cloudflare Worker) for that team's public FPL data. The request passes through Cloudflare, which may log standard request data such as IP address under Cloudflare's privacy policy. We do not store these requests.

If you accept advertising on the consent banner, Google AdSense may set cookies and use identifiers to show and measure ads. See Google's advertising policy. If you decline, no advertising scripts are loaded. You can change your choice any time via "Ad choices" in the footer.

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log visitor IP addresses under GitHub's privacy statement.
