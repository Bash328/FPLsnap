About FPLSnap

Fast, free, no-login tools for Fantasy Premier League managers.

FPLSnap shows fixture difficulty, price changes, player stats, captain picks, injuries, chip timing and live tools, and it loads fast on a phone. Ads keep it free.

It reads only the publicly available FPL API. We never ask for or store your FPL login. Your Team ID and plans stay on your own device.

FPLSnap is an independent tool and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to the Premier League or Fantasy Premier League.
